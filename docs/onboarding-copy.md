# Onboarding Copy — Prototype V2

## Screen 1 — Welcome

### Build it. Run it. See what happens.

This is a fun business simulation where your decisions shape your business.

It is **not a business prediction or validation tool**. Use it to experiment, try different strategies, and see how your choices play out.

**Every decision has consequences. There is no single perfect strategy.**

CTA: **Let's build my business**

## Screen 2 — Your objective

### Your goal

Start and grow a successful business by making smart decisions.

Manage your **cash, customers, staff, reputation, and operations**. Your choices will affect what happens next — sometimes immediately, and sometimes much later.

### How it works

**Make a decision → See the impact → Adapt your strategy → Grow your business**

CTA: **Start my business**
